Physics Overview

Particles: Each particle has mass, charge, valency (max bonds), and size (radius).

Forces:

  • Collision: Strong repulsion when particles overlap (prevents merging)
  • Electromagnetic: Opposite charges attract, like charges repel (inverse-square law)
  • Bonding: Spring forces hold bonded particles at optimal distance
  • Thermal: Random motion proportional to temperature

Temperature: Controls random thermal motion. Higher = more chaotic movement, particles move faster and bonds break more easily.

Energy Map: Visualizes charge density - red areas show high electric potential energy. Useful for seeing charge distributions and bonding zones.
